OmniAct: one policy for a million robot embodiments

A manipulation policy conditions directly on a robot's kinematic graph and predicts relative joint motions by flow matching, trained on a million procedurally generated embodiments. It reaches 63.0% success on held-out embodiment-scene episodes against 17.8% for pi 0.5, and beats GPT-6 Astra 64.7% to 41.1% on its evaluated task families.

A frontier model repairs a quantized network with no tools and no data

Given a 20k-parameter CNN's weights as plain text, Claude Opus 5.5 lifted 4-bit accuracy from 60.4% back to 77.95% by balancing rounding errors inside each 3x3 kernel, 2.8 points short of AdaRound. Handed only the float network, the same models fell back to plain round-to-nearest.

Integral DeltaNet gives linear attention a second memory state

The architecture keeps a fast delta state plus an integral state holding a weighted history of it, combined adaptively at readout with a chunkwise parallel training formulation. At 340M and 1.3B parameters it improves language modelling, commonsense reasoning, retrieval and long-context results over strong recurrent baselines.
