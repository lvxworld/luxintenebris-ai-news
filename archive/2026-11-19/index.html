GateWatch turns an LLM probe into a compact Boolean circuit

GateWatch expresses a probe's entire decision rule as explicit Boolean logic over a few binarized sparse-autoencoder features. Across 24 tasks on Gemma-2-9B it lands within 1.17 points of full 16K-feature SAE probes while cutting probe memory by 99.98%, and inside vLLM it keeps 97-98% of baseline throughput with 1,000 concurrent classifiers.

arXiv · 2026-11-18

AutoPI red-teams LLM agents by technique coverage, not just attack success

Built on a taxonomy of prompt-injection techniques, the black-box, training-free red teamer writes an attack for every sub-technique and reworks the failures with judge feedback. Against Claude Haiku 5.5 it achieved 20-50% higher attack success and up to 400% higher class-level coverage than recent state-of-the-art adaptive attackers.

arXiv · 2026-11-18

Replay, don't resample: consolidating long-horizon RL experts from their own logs

Multi-expert replay policy distillation replays each expert's RL training rollouts through the student in the order the expert learned from them, recomputing the policy gradient on the logged prefixes. It consolidates experts at roughly a seventh of the compute used to train them and beats multi-teacher on-policy distillation from the same experts.

arXiv · 2026-11-18

RePlan-VLA adds progress-aware replanning to long-horizon robot policies

The VLA-agnostic framework monitors the robot's motion to evaluate progress and revises future plans on the fly, preserving the underlying policy with no extra fine-tuning. It lifts success by 21.9% on RoboCasa365 and 10.4% on CALVIN over the next strongest baseline, and was validated on a Franka Research 3 arm.

arXiv · 2026-11-18

Latent reasoning degrades past its useful horizon, a new method finds

Extending latent chain-of-thought to the full preset horizon can hurt reasoning, with later computation mainly reorganizing existing candidates. A lightweight estimator adjusts the latent horizon during inference, improving accuracy by up to 7.2 points while cutting latent computation by up to 29% without retraining.

arXiv · 2026-11-18
