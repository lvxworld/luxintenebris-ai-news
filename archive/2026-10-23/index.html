Research & Papers

05

EpiSEM-Bench: evaluating safety and evidence recovery in LLM agents under missing evidence

When a tool response omits a safety-relevant fact, an agent may carry out a prohibited action even though the user request is benign and every returned statement is true. The benchmark probes exactly this failure mode.

arXiv ·

Causal chain of thought: mitigating attention leakage via context isolation

Chain-of-thought rationales appear to explain model predictions, but there is no guarantee they causally drive the final answer. This work proposes context isolation to make CoT steps genuinely causal.

arXiv ·

BeNice! Closing looping architectures' inference-memory gap via KV-sharing

Looped architectures grow linear KV-cache memory per recurrence during inference. BeNice introduces KV-sharing across recurrences to close the gap while keeping the quality benefits of looping.

arXiv ·

Questioning instrumental convergence in RLVR: checkpoint evidence from OLMo-3

Instrumental convergence is often invoked as a general reason to expect power-seeking subgoals in capable agents. Checkpoint evidence from OLMo-3 training runs complicates that picture.

arXiv ·

Twin-world credit assignment for agentic reinforcement learning

Agentic RL rewards a complete episode, yet the policy needs credit for every decision inside it. The paper proposes twin-world counterfactual comparisons for finer-grained credit.
