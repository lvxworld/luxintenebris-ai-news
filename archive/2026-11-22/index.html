Amazon's world-model bet

Amazon nears a $7.5B deal to acquire world-model startup Decart

Amazon is in advanced talks to acquire Israeli startup Decart for about $7.5 billion, with an announcement possible as soon as next week, according to the Financial Times. The price roughly doubles the $4 billion mark Decart set in May and would hand Amazon a real-time world-model and optimization stack that runs across Nvidia GPUs, Google TPUs and its own Trainium chips.

The Lean FRO and the Mathlib Initiative published a joint statement on the Lean verification of a Riemann Hypothesis proof released on November 20 by Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpoge — a 17-million-line library built with Claude. ForceHarness gives frozen API models a sense of touch, lifting zero-shot arm success from 3.3% to 90%

A harness lets API-only frozen multimodal models use contact force without demonstrations or fine-tuning, via self-terminating force primitives and a physics translation layer that converts force traces into physical quantities. On a real arm with no wrist force sensor it adds 17.5 points on ForceBench and 34.3 points on the new HiddenPhys benchmark.

arXiv · 2026-11-16

VideoNexus splits video perception across sub-agents that report evidence, not transcripts

A training-free framework separates detailed video perception into sub-agents with independent contexts that return compact temporally grounded evidence to a planner. With Gemini 3.8 Flash in both roles it reaches 88.6% on full LVBench at 44% of the cost of built-in agentic processing.

arXiv · 2026-11-16

TCP4D tracks and generates robot tool-center-point motion in one 4D representation

A single 4D scene representation both tracks observed tool-center-point motion in SE(3) and generates future trajectories, with a cross-embodiment pipeline that retargets RGB video into demonstrations. It hits 7.60mm trajectory error on DROID, adapts to a new embodiment from ten demos and reaches 69.19% mean success on RoboTwin.

arXiv · 2026-11-17

M3OPD-Reasoner: an open recipe for post-training multimodal reasoning

Comparing recipes from Qwen3.5-9B across 21 decontaminated benchmarks, multi-teacher on-policy distillation of GSPO teachers wins, matching each teacher in its own domain and beating mixed-training baselines. Stacking MixRL on the distilled student adds further gains without collapse, and all data, code and checkpoints are released.

arXiv · 2026-11-17

Do LLM agents still need to be told how to self-improve?

Give a frozen agent primitives, a compute budget and only a general goal, and sufficiently capable models design their own self-learning algorithm — converging on strategies that match classic self-regulated learning frameworks. The learned text artifacts transfer across game horizons, mechanics and 3D navigation.
