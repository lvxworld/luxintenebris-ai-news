CLAM compiles a session stream into a memory adapter in one forward pass

A recurrent hypernetwork turns activations into a low-rank adapter with no gradient steps and no generation calls, paired with an episodic buffer for verbatim detail. On LoCoMo and LongMemEval-S across three 2-7B models it leads agentic baselines by 1-45 points at 3-151x lower memory-build wall-clock time.

arXiv · 2026-11-16

SilentGuard moves silent-API-failure detection into the tool-calling harness

The authors find silent failures in 11 of 15 public research APIs, with counts inflated up to 691x. Checking every result against invariants cuts wrong answers from 74.0% to 1.7% across seven open models and from 70.2% to 0.5% on three frontier models, where a warning-only fix still leaves 70.1% wrong.

Twenty frontier models shift their accept/reject verdicts on the same papers

Pre-registered tests on ICLR 2027 submissions find the share of papers on the accept side falls 52 and 53 points between two Claude Opus versions and two Gemini versions, and by 43-79 points in six further paired comparisons, with no paper moving up. All twenty models gave a 2 or 3 in 473 of 475 reviews.

arXiv · 2026-11-16

GPRM scores an agent's step against its past and its future

The process reward model judges the problem, prior steps and their evaluations, the current step and future steps jointly, trained with SFT plus GRPO on a four-dimension rubric. With less data and smaller models it beats strong baselines by 7.8 and 5.4 points on PRMBench and ProcessBench and surpasses GPT-4.1 on Agent Error Bench.

arXiv · 2026-11-16

GaugeRoPE takes 5.85x of the KV cache without retraining the model

The paper characterizes when a latent KV representation can serve directly as execution state for pretrained RoPE, then builds the training-free construction it implies. On Mistral-7B it compresses the cache 5.85x at quality close to the full cache and reaches up to 2.48x decode throughput by skipping per-token K/V reconstruction.
