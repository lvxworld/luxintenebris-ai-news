Source: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/cognition-ai-talks-be-valued-75-billion-latest-funding-round-bloomberg-news-2026-11-20/)

### Thinking Machines releases Inkling 2 Mini, a 26B open-weights model that runs on a 32GB laptop

The Apache-2.0 multimodal mixture-of-experts runs its middle 12 layers three times per token, giving 48 layer applications at 5.4B effective active parameters, and ships as a ~20GB NVFP4 checkpoint with a 1M-token context. The lab reports 20.1 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 and calls it the strongest open-weights model under 100B total parameters, while trailing Qwen3.8-27B on multi-turn chat.

Source: [Thinking Machines Lab](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/)

### OpenAI details how it blocks distillation of its frontier models

OpenAI described the adversarial training, input/output classifiers and API-level controls it uses to bind protected reasoning to the conversation that produced it. It says the average attack success rate for recovering protected reasoning fell from 54.0% against GPT-6 Astra to 0.2% against GPT-6.2 Astra.

Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/combatting-adversarial-distillation/)

## Research & Papers

- **[A CPU inside an LLM: exact circuits compiled into pretrained weights with no gradients](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26450)** — Closed-form circuit-grafting primitives compile exact integer arithmetic straight into pretrained weights with zero training examples and no runtime hooks, touching under 0.06% of active non-zeros. On 6-digit arithmetic IBM Granite 4.2 3B reaches 99.17% ALU-prefix accuracy and Qwen 3.5 4B reaches 100.00%, against 42.2% and 61.7% for a 12.8K-sample LoRA. *(arXiv)*
- **[RL post-training in diffusion transformers is mostly activation steering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26627)** — A fixed set of input-independent steering vectors, 0.29M parameters against 18.8M for RL-tuned LoRA, recovers 90.0-96.3% of the reward gains of six RL-tuned image models and up to 111.9% on video. Trained from zero init, the vectors alone beat weight-based RL on OCR text rendering and PickScore. *(arXiv)*
- **[CompAct-Bench shows how long-horizon agents break when their context is compacted](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25673)** — The benchmark inserts compaction boundaries into successful agent trajectories, compresses prior context with the model under test and lets the same executor continue across BrowseComp, SWE-bench and an internal CompanyBench. Frontier Claude and GPT models degrade sharply under aggressive compaction, and the failures trace to distorted task state rather than missing information. *(arXiv)*
- **[WeMM-Video opens a family of bilingual omni-modal video models from 2B to 35B-A3B](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26409)** — The Chinese-English models unify text, image, audio and video under a hybrid-thinking paradigm with separate instruct and step-by-step reasoning modes. Training covered roughly 1.2T multimodal tokens through progressive pretraining and multi-stage post-training. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[Deployment lessons from self-distilled memory: the right memory size belongs to the model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26361)** — A pipeline mines one to three lessons per episode from an agent's own trajectories and consolidates them into guidelines, shipped in altk-evolve and deployed in IBM CUGA and watsonx Orchestrate. On AppWorld, DeepSeek-V3.2 gains 9.5 points with all 191 guidelines, while gpt-oss-120b does better with a selected subset of about 29 than with all 199. *(arXiv)*

## Open Source & Models

- **[Tencent publishes the full 770B Hy4 weights, moving its flagship from API-only to open](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hy4)** — Tencent released Hy4, Hy4-FP8 and a pre-trained base with 774B parameters, activating 49B text and 1.23B vision parameters per token across a 1,048,576-token context. The release adds vision and computer use, pairs gated sparse attention with a 3.3B speculative drafter, and ships under the Tencent Hy License rather than an OSI licence. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[Thinking Machines ships a variable-loop checkpoint so outsiders can test reasoning monitorability](https://huggingface.co/thinkingmachines/TML-d24-varloop-research)** — TML-d24-varloop-research accepts between one and five passes through the looped middle layers instead of a fixed three, adding about 1.0B effective active parameters per pass. The lab reports the no-chain-of-thought time horizon rising from 16 seconds at one pass to 37 seconds at three, and flags the weights as a research artifact post-trained at three passes. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[Unsloth quantises Inkling 2 Mini to 7.76GB on release day](https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Inkling-2-Mini-GGUF)** — Dynamic GGUFs span UD-IQ1_S at 7.76GB, with 0.0911 mean KLD against the Q8 build and 89.4% same top token, up to UD-Q8_K_XL at 22.87GB. Because the model is already native NVFP4 and MXFP8, the 1-bit and 2-bit builds are the first path under the original 20GB footprint. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[Arena's revised alignment index shows the open-weight gap sits in agent behaviour, not benchmarks](https://ashutoshai.com/ai-compass/2026/11/20/arena-alignment-index-agent-leaderboard-update/)** — Arena regraded live user agent sessions for unauthorised actions, deceptive completion and false attribution, and OpenAI took all seven top individual slots. Kimi K3.1 Pro is the highest open-weight entry at #9 with 78.2, but the next open model falls to #26 — a gap that matters for anyone putting an open model behind autonomous tool use. *(AI Compass)*

## Tools & Startups

- **[DreamCoder arrives: a GUI-first coding harness from the author of Mastering Emacs](https://www.masteringemacs.org/article/announcing-dreamcoder)** — Mickey Petersen shipped DreamCoder after 15 months of work, with MCP support, backgroundable sub-agents and shell commands, skills, hooks, and importers for Pi, OpenCode and Codex configs. It reached 170 points on Hacker News on announcement day. *(Mastering Emacs)*
- **[OpenComputer ships an Apache-2.0 alternative to Devin](https://github.com/diggerhq/opencomputer)** — Digger's agent gets pulled into a Slack thread, works on its own rootless-Podman computer and returns a pull request. The project claims its benchmark opens PRs 3.3x faster than Devin, and it can be self-hosted on a single VM or run on OpenComputer Cloud. *(GitHub)*
- **[Qwen3 TTS lands on Together AI with first audio in 97ms](https://www.together.ai/blog/qwen3-tts)** — Qwen's open text-to-speech model is now on serverless inference and dedicated endpoints at $20 per 1M characters, with nine preset voices and ten languages per voice. Together says its serving stack reaches first audio in as little as 97ms over HTTP streaming or WebSocket. *(Together AI)*
- **[Saparo tops Product Hunt with a shopping agent that stacks every discount](https://www.producthunt.com/products/saparo)** — Saparo searches US stores in the user's size, tests promo codes, cash back and card offers together, then ranks options by estimated total cost including shipping and tax. It took the top spot on Product Hunt's November 20 leaderboard with 312 points. *(Product Hunt)*
- **[Kong's Volcano agent cloud reaches general availability](https://volcano.dev/blog/introducing-volcano-agent-cloud)** — Volcano hosts agents, apps, branchable Postgres, durable workflows, auth and file storage, deployable from Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or a browser builder. Its LavaFlow system continuously finds issues and proposes fixes; the launch drew 108 points on Product Hunt. *(Volcano by Kong)*
