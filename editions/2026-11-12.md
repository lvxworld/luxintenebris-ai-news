## Lead Memory Landgrab: Samsung pulls its Onyang HBM plant forward a year and lifts Chungcheong investment to 144 trillion won

Samsung broke ground on its Onyang HBM packaging campus in Asan on November 12 and moved the start of operations up by a year to June 2028, citing surging AI server memory demand. ## Top stories

### Fractile raises $720M Series C at about $7B valuation for AI inference chips

The London chip startup took $720 million led by Thrive Capital, Founders Fund, Lightspeed and Redpoint, with the UK government's Sovereign AI Fund participating, at over £5 billion. It builds inference silicon with DRAM stacked on-chip.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/with-720m-round-britains-fractile-gets-a-leg-up-in-the-race-for-ai-chips/)

### Runway releases Praxis-1, a 27B open-weights world action model

Praxis-1 is a mixture-of-experts diffusion transformer that predicts video and robot actions together, shipped under Apache 2.0 with inference and fine-tuning code. Runway reports 46.2% on NVIDIA's RoboLab-120, ahead of FLUX 3 Action at 42.9% and pi0.5 at 28.0%.

Source: [Runway](https://runway.com/news/company-news/praxis-1-is-now-available)

### DeepMind's AlphaGenome 2 reads DNA one cell type at a time

The conditional sequence model predicts how variants affect gene expression in specific cell types and states, trained on more than 100 million single-cell profiles across 68 tissues. It finds nearly twice as many expression-altering variants as its predecessor, with API access for non-commercial research.

Source: [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-2-deciphering-how-cells-read-dna-to-better-understand-disease/)

### Google opens a Gemini Decisions API that answers up to 1,000 questions in one pass

The API handles yes/no, multiple-choice and rubric-scoring questions across text, images, audio, video and PDFs in a single call, pitched as a calibrated alternative to prompting a general model repeatedly. It runs on a dedicated Gemini Decision 1 model.

Source: [Google (The Keyword)](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/)

## Research & Papers

- **[Tree-Ising repairs language models with bit-level Ising edits](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.14210)** — Casts weight repair as sparse discrete optimization, splitting it into per-row QUBO problems solved by GPU simulated annealing. On the two layers MIT's GPT-6 Astra agent targeted it reaches 54.6% on MATH-500 versus 51.7%, flipping 2.2 to 2.3 times fewer bits with four forward passes and zero LLM tokens. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[Are dense rewards worth their tokens in GRPO?](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.13560)** — A matched-compute protocol equalizing tokens and GPU-hours across 1,668 runs reverses the usual ranking of dense process rewards. On Qwen2.5-1.5B the Monte-Carlo reward leads per update but ends 14.3 points lower at equal tokens and 7.8 points lower at equal training time. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[Robot memory policies treat elapsed time as progress](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.14488)** — Memory-augmented VLA policies index memory by clock time, so a task-irrelevant pause reads as progress and triggers early stopping: success drops from 47/50 to 16/50 on one RoboMME counting task. Event-based memory indexing recovers half or more of the loss without retraining. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[A frozen robot policy learns new tasks by rehearsing them in a simulator](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.12885)** — WICL has a frozen model rehearse a demonstrated task in simulation and write the resulting world feedback into context instead of weights. RoboScript reaches 83.4% mean success on all 50 RoboTwin 2.0 tasks from a single demonstration with no gradient updates, and transfers to a physical dual-arm robot. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[LLMs can build valid world models that are still wrong](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14419)** — A 44-scenario benchmark tests whether five LLMs can write formal world models as partially observable stochastic games. 134 of 135 generations pass a strict mechanical audit, but mean structural F1 against authored references is only 0.366 and just 28 match exactly. *(arXiv)*

## Hardware & Robotics

- **[Taiwan's AI server supply chain posts record October revenue, up 63.8%](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20261111VL202/ai-server-supply-chain-revenue-sales-taiwan.html)** — Combined October sales at 49 Taiwanese AI server suppliers rose 63.8% year on year to a record NT$3.22 trillion, about $98.2 billion, according to DIGITIMES' tracker. Demand holds up despite the wider chip sell-off. *(DIGITIMES)*
- **[AMD lays out three generations of EPYC on TSMC 2nm](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20261111PD259/amd-2nm-tsmc-data-center-cpu.html)** — AMD's data center CPU roadmap now runs three generations on the 2nm process, framed around inference, agent collaboration and real-time data processing. It follows the November 10 launch of 6th Gen EPYC 'Venice' SP7 chips on Zen 6. *(DIGITIMES)*
- **[Serve Robotics opens its delivery fleet to outside developers](https://www.therobotreport.com/serve-robotics-opens-robot-delivery-network-for-others-to-build-on/)** — An AV Delivery Developer Portal with a public API and sandbox lets developers and AI assistants summon robots from a fleet of more than 2,000 sidewalk units. Live robot access still requires Serve's approval. *(The Robot Report)*
- **[Berkeley and NVIDIA let coding agents program industrial robots without training data](https://www.therobotreport.com/can-ai-agents-program-industrial-robots-without-training-data-berkeley-says-yes/)** — Graph-as-Policy and the VABAR benchmark have teams of AI agents write, simulate and refine robot programs instead of collecting datasets. The work was presented at CoRL 2026 and is being evaluated by Bosch Research and Ambi Robotics. *(The Robot Report)*
- **[Intel extends 18A into AI PCs with Wildcat Lake](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20261111PD211/intel-ai-pc-processor-packaging-tsmc.html)** — The Intel Core Series 3 processor, codenamed Wildcat Lake, targets mainstream consumer and commercial notebooks and widens Intel's platform pitch for agentic AI at high volume. *(DIGITIMES)*

## Tools & Startups

- **[Databricks relaunches Genie as an always-on enterprise agent](https://www.databricks.com/company/newsroom/press-releases/databricks-launches-new-always-genie-autonomous-ai-complex)** — The second-generation Genie owns long-running goals, delegates to subagents and escalates through a priority inbox and email. It adds Genie Ontology for cross-system context and Genie Search across more than 100 sources including Drive, Jira and Confluence. *(Databricks)*
- **[AWS publishes an emulator written entirely by autonomous agents](https://github.com/aws/aws-emulator)** — The local AWS emulator's codebase was produced by thousands of agents that write tests, find issues and fix code without human review. Source is on GitHub under CC0 and keeps updating; the story hit 775 points on Hacker News. *(GitHub (AWS))*
- **[Atuin Threads syncs and resumes agent sessions across machines](https://blog.atuin.sh/threads-agent-sessions/)** — From Atuin 18.24, Claude Code and Codex sessions become searchable and synced like shell history. `atuin resume` continues a task elsewhere or forks it into another harness with context intact. *(Atuin)*
- **[n8n ships Nod, an open-source personal agent with its own computer](https://github.com/n8n-io/nod)** — Nod is an Apache-2.0 agent that runs locally on the model of your choice and drives its own browser to chase invoices and cancel trials, asking before it acts. It is built in TypeScript on Fastify, React and PostgreSQL, with MCP and Composio integrations. *(GitHub (n8n))*
- **[Huawei releases MACaQ, a model-agnostic GGUF quantizer](https://github.com/huawei-csl/MACaQ)** — Huawei's Computing Systems Lab published an Apache-2.0 quantization method for local inference covering text, image, video and audio models through llama.cpp, vLLM, Diffusers and PyTorch. *(GitHub (Huawei CSL))*
