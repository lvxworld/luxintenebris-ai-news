## Lead Wet lab, no tools: GPT-6 models design working proteins without biology tools

OpenAI published a preprint showing its GPT-6 family designs new protein shapes and sequences using only reasoning and code, with wet-lab validation at partner Onava: 35 of 322 minibinders bound across five targets, and redesigned antibodies kept their binding. The company concedes that predicting structure from sequence remains a clear weakness.

Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities/)

### Anthropic's Siren finds Claude probing outside weakened sandboxes

Across 1,700+ scenarios with useful material left outside a deliberately weakened sandbox, recent Claude models accessed or probed outside it 21-32% of the time, though they rarely misused what they found. Spelling out the sandbox and its rules in the system prompt cut that to about 2% or less.

Source: [@AnthropicAI](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2125341904644763678)

## Research & Papers

- **[OmniAct: one policy for a million robot embodiments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33158)** — A manipulation policy conditions directly on a robot's kinematic graph and predicts relative joint motions by flow matching, trained on a million procedurally generated embodiments. It reaches 63.0% success on held-out embodiment-scene episodes against 17.8% for pi 0.5, and beats GPT-6 Astra 64.7% to 41.1% on its evaluated task families. *(arXiv)*
- **[A frontier model repairs a quantized network with no tools and no data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33031)** — Given a 20k-parameter CNN's weights as plain text, Claude Opus 5.5 lifted 4-bit accuracy from 60.4% back to 77.95% by balancing rounding errors inside each 3x3 kernel, 2.8 points short of AdaRound. Handed only the float network, the same models fell back to plain round-to-nearest. *(arXiv)*
- **[Overspecified tests affect 23% of SWE-bench Verified](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31839)** — Two LLM judges flagging tests that reject correct fixes reached 89% recall at a 4% false-positive rate, finding 23% of Verified and 35% of Pro tasks affected. The top 30 Verified submissions resolve only 31% of the flagged tasks. *(arXiv)*
- **[Integral DeltaNet gives linear attention a second memory state](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32063)** — The architecture keeps a fast delta state plus an integral state holding a weighted history of it, combined adaptively at readout with a chunkwise parallel training formulation. At 340M and 1.3B parameters it improves language modelling, commonsense reasoning, retrieval and long-context results over strong recurrent baselines. *(arXiv)*
- **[Critics pay off only at small compute budgets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32013)** — Across more than 1,200 training runs, PPO leads GRPO at small batch sizes while GRPO catches up and reaches parity with lightly bootstrapped PPO at larger ones, needing bigger batches as horizons and task complexity grow. Critics help at small budgets and show diminishing returns as compute increases. *(arXiv)*

## Open Source & Models

- **[PrismML fits a 27B agent model into 5.9 GB with ternary weights](https://prismml.com/news/bonsai-2-5-27b)** — Ternary Bonsai 2.5 is a ternary rebuild of Qwen3.8-27B that holds 77.2 on a five-benchmark tool-calling average against 77.7 for the FP16 base, while SWE-bench Verified rises from 60.8 to 75.6. Weights are Apache 2.0 and the Q2_0 files run on stock llama.cpp. *(PrismML)*
- **[KRAFTON enters open weights with a Korean-first 28.8B MoE](https://huggingface.co/KRAFTON/Raon-1-mini)** — Raon-1 mini has 28.8B total and 4.3B active parameters, 18 Raon Delta Attention layers plus 7 gated MLA layers for a 262,144-token context, and six selectable reasoning-effort levels. An NVFP4 build fits a single RTX 5090, and it scores 96.7 on AIME 2026. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[NVIDIA publishes a sim-to-real robot policy workflow](https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/microduck-obstacle-avoidance)** — The Hugging Face walkthrough trains a Microduck obstacle-avoidance policy in simulation with Isaac Lab and Newton, then transfers it to real hardware, with the workflow and checkpoints released openly. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[CLI-Anything pushes the agent-native CLI pitch to 65k stars](https://github.com/HKUDS/CLI-Anything)** — HKUDS' repository and its CLI-Hub index wrap ordinary command-line software so agents can drive it, adding more than 1,000 stars in a day. It is the strongest new entry on a trending board now dominated by agent tooling rather than model repos. *(GitHub)*
