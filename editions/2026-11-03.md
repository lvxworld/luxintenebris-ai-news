## Lead Marvell's optical bet: Marvell to acquire Lumentum for $115B in an all-stock deal

Marvell signed a definitive agreement to buy optical chip and networking maker Lumentum in an all-stock transaction with an implied enterprise value of $115 billion, roughly $1,132 per share and a 23.9% premium. The companies frame it as a bet that AI infrastructure is moving from copper to integrated optical connectivity, combining Marvell's switching and custom XPU work with Lumentum's lasers, transceivers and optical circuit switching. Closing is expected in H1 2028, pending shareholder and regulatory approval.

Source: [TechCrunch / TechPowerUp](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/in-one-of-the-largest-deals-in-tech-history-marvell-buys-lumentum-for-115b/)

## Top stories

### GPT-6 Astra edits model weights directly in "weight-space programming" result

A thread by MIT CSAIL's Rikiya Takehi reports that GPT-6 Astra read and patched raw model weights using only forward passes, editing 0.016% of Qwen3.5-0.8B to lift MATH-500 from 7.9% to 48.1% and improve IFEval roughly 3x. The edited model, a blog post and a benchmark for weight-space reasoning were published alongside.

Source: [Rikiya Takehi](https://x.com/rikiyatakehi/status/2117313742891327994)

### Salesforce signs definitive agreement to acquire Cresta for voice agents

Salesforce said it signed a definitive agreement to acquire contact-center AI company Cresta, folding its autonomous voice agents and conversation intelligence into Service Cloud and Agentforce. Reports citing sources put the price near $2.7 billion, with closing expected in the first quarter of Salesforce's fiscal 2028.

Source: [Salesforce](https://x.com/salesforce/status/2117361573492605148)

### Physical Intelligence reportedly raising $1B at a $20B valuation

Bloomberg-sourced reports say robotics foundation-model company Physical Intelligence is in talks to raise at least $1 billion at roughly a $20 billion valuation, with existing backers Avenir and Lux Capital in discussions to lead. The round would extend a steep climb from about $2.4 billion in late 2024, despite no meaningful revenue.

Source: [Lukas Ziegler](https://x.com/lukas_m_ziegler/status/2117342105194422426)

### OpenAI launches Grid Protectors to make compute campuses grid-flexible

OpenAI announced the Grid Protectors Program, a national effort with utilities, regulators and policymakers to design data center campuses that cut electricity demand when the grid is strained. The company says it will pay the full infrastructure and electric-service costs for its planned Effingham County, Georgia campus, and can supply power from on-site generation or storage when needed.

Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/grid-protectors-program/)

## Research & Papers

- **[TAC-CoT swaps textual reasoning for latent segments, cutting forward passes up to 6.8x](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01926)** — Replaces textual reasoning traces with compact latent segments while keeping text only for tool calls and answers, improving accuracy by up to 8.8 points on agentic math and QA benchmarks while cutting model forward passes by up to 6.8x. *(arXiv)*
- **[Hidden tool updates slip malicious code past agent harnesses 97.6% of the time](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01976)** — ETUDE hides malicious functionality in updates to previously benign tools so it executes outside the model context; across seven frameworks including Gemini CLI, Codex CLI and Claude Code it reaches 97.6% attack success while keeping 95.9% of legitimate tasks completing. *(arXiv)*
- **[Fewer than 11% of underspecified queries trigger a follow-up question](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01878)** — Across seven LLMs from 8B to frontier, fewer than 11% of underspecified queries prompt a clarifying question. The authors release VagueBench and DialogGRPO, an offline RL method over a dialogue-state DAG that raises elicitation of missing information above 88% of vague queries. *(arXiv)*
- **[A bandit meta-controller lifts agent first-attempt success by 20.5 points](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01747)** — OMCE adds a contextual-bandit controller that routes among four cognitive actions — direct, episode, guide and distill. Across four online adaptation benchmarks and four backbones it improves first-attempt success by up to 20.5 points while cutting per-task tokens by 5.7x to 62.5x. *(arXiv)*
- **[LegoEnv builds 5,465 agent environments by recombining modular parts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01834)** — Recombining reusable atoms across 223 applications yields 5,465 verified environments; one evolution round lifts the share of solvable environments from 71.6% to 88.3%, and SFT on the corpus raises Qwen3.6-27B from 16.0% to 32.0% on AutomationBench. *(arXiv)*

## Open Source & Models

- **[Deep Principle releases Hydrogen-1.0, an open model post-trained on lab notebooks](https://x.com/DeepPrinciple/status/2117249008972550447)** — The Hangzhou-based AI-for-materials company post-trained Hydrogen-1.0 from GLM-5.3 on laboratory notebooks and full discovery trajectories rather than published papers. It reports 38.3% on FrontierScience-Research against 33.3% for GPT-6 Astra and 93.0% on GPQA-Diamond, with weights released openly. *(Deep Principle)*
- **[Strata v0.2.10 serves 128K context on 8GB of VRAM](https://github.com/Niko1221/Strata)** — The C++ local inference engine ships one-click Windows and Linux installs and exposes OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible endpoints on localhost. The v0.2.10 release gets long contexts onto 8GB cards with Qwen3.8 variants and pushes decode on large MoE models into the 20+ tok/s range. *(GitHub)*
- **[Colibri runs frontier MoE models in pure C by streaming experts from disk](https://x.com/lunkertw/status/2117261837557469590)** — JustVugg's zero-dependency engine bets that the model should not fit in RAM at all: experts stream from disk as the router selects them, which puts an out-of-reach frontier MoE on hardware people already own. The cost is that storage I/O becomes the new bottleneck. *(@lunkertw)*
- **[Meituan's LongCat-2.5 pushes open weights to 1.6T for long agentic coding](https://x.com/michaelnemtsev/status/2117439549240439084)** — LongCat-2.5 is a 1.6T-parameter model tuned for long coding and agentic workflow sessions, with open weights described as imminent. It joins Mistral Large 4 and Kimi K3.1 in a cluster of trillion-scale releases arriving in the same window. *(@michaelnemtsev)*
- **[The trillion-parameter open release wave is colliding with export-control talk](https://x.com/sanchitmonga22/status/2117272575072186688)** — The same week that Mistral, Moonshot and Meituan pushed or promised weights in the 1.6T-2.8T range, discussion turned to potential export controls on open weights above roughly 2T parameters. At this scale, releasing weights is releasing infrastructure, and the argument that open models are inherently ungovernable weakens once a checkpoint needs a cluster to serve. *(@sanchitmonga22)*

## Hardware & Robotics
