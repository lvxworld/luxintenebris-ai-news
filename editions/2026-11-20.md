## Lead Acquisition: Palo Alto Networks to buy AI startup Applied Compute for $4.5B in stock

The cybersecurity giant agreed to acquire Applied Compute, a 2025 startup founded by former OpenAI researchers that helps enterprises post-train, deploy and run open-weight models. The all-stock deal, announced alongside Q1 earnings, values the company at $4.5B just weeks after a $3.25B round, and Palo Alto shares rose about 8.5% after hours.

Source: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/palo-alto-networks-earnings-applied-compute.html)

## Top stories

### Nous Research brings Hermes Agent into Microsoft Teams and Azure

A partnership with Microsoft puts Hermes Agent inside Microsoft Teams, the Microsoft Store and Azure, with agent workloads running in isolated Azure Container Apps sandboxes. Enterprises can run Hermes in their own Azure tenant for data-residency reasons.

Source: [@NousResearch](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2123535954946257096)

### Google ships Genie 4 to the Gemini API and AI Studio

One day after launch, Genie 4 is available to developers through the Gemini API and AI Studio, generating continuous 720p worlds at 24fps with synchronized audio from text or a reference image. It is the first model on the new Live Interactions API, with real-time navigation and SynthID watermarking.

Source: [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/genie-4-in-gemini-api/)

### How OpenAI's Performance Factory keeps ChatGPT fast

OpenAI detailed an internal Codex-powered system that automatically investigates and fixes performance regressions in ChatGPT. Its first Regression Workflow has tracked more than 1,700 investigations and root-caused over 600 cases in just over three months.

Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/how-openai-performance-factory-keeps-chatgpt-fast)

### Google partners with Blue Origin on Project Suncatcher heavy-lift launch

Google signed Blue Origin for the first heavy-lift launch of its space-compute moonshot, targeted for Q1 2027 on a New Glenn rocket. The satellites, each roughly the capacity of a data center rack, would go to sun-synchronous orbit to tap orbital solar power for AI infrastructure.

Source: [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-research/blue-origin-project-suncatcher-partnership/)

## Research & Papers

- **[FastFrames: Apple pushes video generation to interactive latency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23226)** — Apple's autoregressive video diffusion family replaces VAE decoding and KV-cache updates, which account for over half of inference time. The 1.3B variant runs at 36 ms per frame on a single B200 while matching a model nearly 8x slower. *(arXiv)*
- **[Benchleaking is accelerating: base models regurgitate hidden test items](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21315)** — Few-shot continuation on 20 benchmarks shows ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base leaking 96% of HumanEval problems with reference implementations, versus 9% for DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Base. A proposed Anti-Hack score costs the worst offender 16.6 points on average. *(arXiv)*
- **[Sage S2.1 Flash: a 320B MoE with encoders for molecules, genes and spectra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21072)** — The general-purpose MoE with dedicated molecular, biological and spectral encoders ranks first among five baselines on OfficeQA Pro (72.2), MLE-bench Lite (90.3 HumanRank), Harvey LAB and SpreadsheetBench 2. *(arXiv)*
- **[Weight-space programming doesn't need Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22055)** — Three frontier LLMs asked to design their own architecture for few-shot tasks all converge on sparse tables queried by a fixed procedure, beating the most accurate Transformer they wrote with about 1,800x fewer parameters. *(arXiv)*
- **[Multi-Branch Transformer cuts KV-cache storage and speeds decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19800)** — Branching a Transformer into parallel block sequences fused before shared final layers beats sequential Transformers at matched compute in 0.8B-2.1B pretraining, cutting KV-cache storage up to 34% and raising throughput up to 51.4% on one H200. *(arXiv)*

## Open Source & Models

- **[Tencent releases Hy4 flash open weights: 224B MoE, 12B active, 1M context](https://technode.com/2026/11/19/tencent-releases-hy4-flash-model-weights-for-local-deployment/)** — Two days after the apps-and-API launch, Tencent shipped three checkpoints — standard, FP8 and pretrained base — on Hugging Face, ModelScope and GitCode under the Tencent Hy License. Community ExLlamaV3 and GGUF quantizations were online within 48 hours. *(TechNode)*
- **[RadixArk's DaisyRose-27B: Qwen3.8-27B post-trained with RL only, Apache 2.0](https://huggingface.co/blog/RadixArk/daisyrose-27b-pushing-the-frontier-of-post-trainin)** — The first RadixArk model was trained purely with reinforcement learning on about 1,200 long-horizon agent tasks — no SFT, no distillation — at a cost of roughly 63,000 GPU-hours. Terminal-Bench 4.1 pass@1 rises from 5.6% to 12.0%, with BF16, FP8 and NVFP4 weights. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[Community squeezes Hy4 flash to ExLlamaV3 within 48 hours of weight release](https://huggingface.co/OpensourceWTF/Hy4-flash-exl3)** — A community EXL3 quantization appeared the day after Tencent published the weights, alongside five official GGUF builds and reports of fitting the resident part of the 220B MoE into roughly 49GB of memory. *(Hugging Face)*
